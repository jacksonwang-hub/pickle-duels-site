## Licensing rules for this site

This is a commercial site, so only these are acceptable:

- **Unsplash License** or **Pexels License** — commercial use, no attribution required
  (we credit anyway, as a courtesy and for provenance).
- **CC0 / Public Domain**.
- **CC BY** with attribution recorded below.

Do **not** use anything marked **NC** (non-commercial) or **ND** (no derivatives),
and do not hotlink — download the file into `public/images/`.
