This policy applies to three things we operate:

  • This website — our marketing site.
  • The Pickle Duels app — our player app for iOS and Android.
  • VenueOS — our pickleball tournament management software for clubs and organizers, at venue.pickleduels.com.

This website

We do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking on this website. The site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and loads no scripts, fonts, or images from other companies. Browsing these pages involves no tracking of any kind.

VenueOS

VenueOS is used by clubs and tournament organizers. Two different groups of people have information in it, and our role differs for each.

If you are an organizer

When you create a VenueOS account we collect the information needed to set it up and identify you. Accounts are secured through Clerk, our authentication provider, who processes your sign-in credentials on our behalf. You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Google or Facebook. [PLACEHOLDER: any other organizer account information collected, such as phone number or billing details.]

If you registered for a tournament

When you sign up for a tournament run on VenueOS, the organizer collects your registration details — including your name and email address — and we store and process that information on their behalf. The organizer decides what to collect and how to use it. If you want your registration information corrected or removed, contact the club or organizer running the event, or contact us and we will help put you in touch.

Emails about future tournaments

VenueOS lets organizers email people who registered for their past tournaments to invite them to upcoming ones, using the email addresses collected at registration. These emails come from the organizer running the event, not from us.
