Who we are

{% include components/founder-card.html name="Huyen Hoang" role="Co-founder" photo="/images/founder-huyen.png" alt="Illustrated portrait of Huyen Hoang, smiling, in a grey t-shirt" photoLabel="[PLACEHOLDER: photo of Huyen playing pickleball]" bio="A lifelong athlete and community builder, I've spent my career creating products that bring people together. After one chaotic pickleball tournament, I knew there had to be a better way! Today, I'm building tools to simplify the pickleball experience while also running a local, family-friendly pickleball club. My goal is to make the sport more accessible, organized, and welcoming for everyone." %} {% include components/founder-card.html name="Jackson Wang" role="Co-founder" photo="/images/founder-jackson.png" alt="Illustrated portrait of Jackson Wang wearing glasses, on a mint background" photoLabel="[PLACEHOLDER: photo of Jackson playing pickleball]" bio="I've never been a natural athlete, and coordination has never been my superpower. But I love a good challenge, a little friendly competition, and the thrill of unlocking an achievement. With a background in product design and gamification, I'm inspired by products like Peloton, Strava, and Duolingo that make progress fun and rewarding. My mission is to bring that same magic to sports and fitness, helping people like me find the motivation to get moving, stay active, and have fun along the way!" %}
